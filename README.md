# Netflix-Dashboard
This interactive Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the Netflix content library, offering insights into the distribution, trends, and characteristics of movies and TV shows available on the platform.
# Key Features:
📅 Content Over Time
Visualizes the total number of movies and TV shows added to Netflix each year from 2008 to 2021, highlighting the growth trajectory of the platform.

🍿 Movie & TV Show Distribution
A pie chart breaks down the proportion of content types, revealing that Movies make up approximately 69.69% of the library, while TV Shows account for 30.31%.

🎬 Ratings Breakdown
A bar chart displaying content ratings providing a quick glance at the audience classification distribution.

🌍 Global Content Availability
A world map visualizes the total number of titles available by country, indicating Netflix's content presence across different regions.

🏆 Top 10 Genres
Highlights the most popular genres on Netflix based on the number of distinct titles, such as Documentaries, Stand-Up Comedy, Kids' TV, and International Dramas.

📂 Filter Options
Users can filter the dataset by:

Type (All or Movie or TV Show)

Title (All or Movie or TV Show Title)

# Tools Used:
Tableau for data visualization
Netflix Dataset (sourced from Kaggle)
